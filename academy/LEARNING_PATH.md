# One learning path (design, not yet built)

Principle: one path, one "Next" button, short beats, and never more than
about three minutes of the same format in a row.

## A lesson = five beats (about 8 to 10 minutes)
1. **Watch**: a 30 to 90 s explainer (fact-checked, see SCRIPT_REVIEW.md).
2. **Spot it**: a 60 to 90 s micro-game on that topic (swipe red flags, pick
   the odd transaction, name the owner). Fast, forgiving.
3. **Decide**: one scenario with a real choice and a short consequence
   ("what would you do, and what happens if you are wrong").
4. **Check**: four questions, pass mark 3 of 4 (default, configurable).
5. **Read the law**: the verbatim article, one paragraph, with a link.

## A season = three lessons plus a Shift
After every third lesson comes a **Shift**: a 10 to 15 minute Desk or
Workstation day that mixes what you just learned with earlier topics.
Order: Context, AML basics and risk-based approach, CDD and KYC (Shift 1);
Beneficial ownership, PEPs, Transaction monitoring (Shift 2); Sanctions,
STRs, DORA/MiCA primer (Shift 3, capstone).

## Rhythm rules
- A **warm-up** opens every session: two minutes replaying the questions you
  got wrong last time (spaced repetition).
- The hub shows one row, a progress line and one big **Next** button.
  Everything else (rank, skills, practice rooms) sits below.
- Time picker (15, 30, 60 minutes) builds the session from warm-up, next
  beats and, if time allows, a Shift.
- Light streaks and ranks stay; no punishing mechanics.

## Mapping existing games to topics
Laundromat: placement and layering. Ownership: beneficial ownership and PEPs.
Desk: monitoring, sanctions, STR drafting. Workstation: Shifts and capstone.

## Product tie-ins
- Completion records feed training evidence in the Compliance workspace
  (module, date, score; anonymous by default).
- Managers see assignments and completion; the MLRO can assign a path to a
  role (analyst, manager, board) and switch modules on or off.
- Later: lessons generated from the firm's own policies, with the same
  script gate, citations and human approval (see ROADMAP.md).
