## Storyboard

World: 1600 x 900 logical units per frame, wider world for the camera to travel (see
`academy/films/README.md`). Colour temperature: **warm** (#FFB454) = not yet understood / risk;
**cool** (#78A9FF) = assessed, checked, explained. HUD top right for the whole film:
`FICTIONAL EXAMPLE` over `Brindle Pay` with a temperature dot that moves from warm to cool as the
firm understands its risks, and back to warm in the risks beat.

### S1 · Hook · 0:00 to 0:05

- **Camera**: fade from black (0.35 s). Tight on a single card (z 1.7), slow drift out to z 1.2.
- **Visual**: a prepaid card drawn in line art (path-length draw, as Café Aurore in film 01). A
  dashed border line of a country slides under it; the card crosses the line. A faint warm glow
  appears where the card lands.
- **Kinetic type**: `New card.` · `New country.` · `New risks?` (word rise, 0.08 s stagger;
  `risks?` in warm). Under the card, mono: `Brindle Pay · fictional e-money firm`.
- **Caption C1**: One example scenario: Brindle Pay, a fictional e-money firm, takes its prepaid card to a new country. The situation: a known product in a new market.
- **Sound**: pulse at 0.15 s.

### S2 · Four habits · 0:05 to 0:13

- **Camera**: pulls back and right (cam ease) to frame four cards in a row, z 1.0.
- **Visual**: four cards deal in (outBack, 0.12 s apart): **KNOW** the customer · **WATCH** what
  they do · **REPORT** suspicions → FIU · **KEEP** the records. Then each card gets its own small
  cool comet circling it, **all four at once and without stopping**: the duties run side by side,
  not one after the other. No stage numbers on the cards (see README, "lessons from film 01").
- **Kinetic type**: header eyebrow `A COMMON WAY TO TEACH IT` (mono, char split) with accent bar;
  headline `Four habits. All at once.`
- **Caption C2**: A common way to teach the everyday duties is four habits: know your customer, watch what they do, report suspicions to the Financial Intelligence Unit, and keep the records. They run side by side, not in a line.
- **Sound**: one tock per card; soft loop tone while the comets circle.

### S3 · Your own risks · 0:13 to 0:27

- **Camera**: the four cards shrink to the centre (z 1.0 → 0.85 with a 0.18 dip) and become the
  hub of a **radar**: six spokes, mono labels. A sweep line turns once every 2.4 s.
- **Visual (0:13 to 0:20)**: spokes labelled `ACTIVITIES` · `PRODUCTS` · `TRANSACTIONS` ·
  `DELIVERY CHANNELS` · `CUSTOMERS` · `GEOGRAPHICAL ZONES`. As the sweep passes, blips appear;
  two are warm: `Prepaid card` on PRODUCTS and `New country` on GEOGRAPHICAL ZONES.
- **Visual (0:20 to 0:27)**: the sweep stops on the two warm blips; they cool one notch as a
  document card slides up from below: `BUSINESS-WIDE RISK ASSESSMENT`, with three lines that type
  on: `Drawn up by: compliance officer` · `Approved by: management body (management function)` ·
  `Documented · kept up to date · available to supervisors`. A small stamp lands on the card:
  `BEFORE LAUNCH`.
- **Kinetic type**: header `01 · YOUR OWN RISKS`, headline `Know your own risks.`
- **Caption C3** (0:13 to 0:20): Underneath sits the firm's own business-wide risk assessment: its activities, products, transactions, delivery channels, customers and geographical zones.
- **Caption C4** (0:20 to 0:27): Before a new product goes live, or an existing one reaches a new country, the firm identifies and assesses the risks and takes measures to manage them. The compliance officer draws up the assessment; the management body approves it.
- **Sound**: low sweep hum; a light tone per blip; stamp thud at the stamp.

### S4 · Effort follows risk · 0:27 to 0:40

- **Camera**: radar slides to the top-left corner as a small inset (it stays on screen: the
  customer decisions "take it into account"). Camera recentres on two customer cards, z 1.05.
- **Visual (0:27 to 0:34)**: a horizontal dial appears between the cards, cool on the left, warm
  on the right, labels at the ends: `LOWER RISK · may simplify` and `INCREASED RISK · enhanced
  measures`. Two customer cards drop in:
  - `Customer A · fictional` / `Salaried · small top-ups from own bank account`
  - `Customer B · fictional` / `Company set up last month · asks for high limits · funds from several countries`

  A thin cool line runs from the radar inset to each card (the individual analysis takes the
  business-wide assessment into account). Each card's needle settles: A left of centre, B right.
  A mono note under the dial: `Rating: Brindle Pay's own analysis in this example`.
- **Visual (0:34 to 0:40)**: check blocks stack on each card. Both cards get the **same two
  base blocks**, `Due diligence` and `Monitoring`, so no measure is shown as skipped; A's base
  blocks are visibly thinner (lighter extent), B's are full height. Then three warm blocks rise on
  top of B only: `Source of funds` · `Senior management approval` · `Closer monitoring`. On A, the
  `Monitoring` block pulses once with the label `still enough to spot the unusual`.
- **Kinetic type**: header `02 · EFFORT FOLLOWS RISK`, headline `More risk, more checks.`
- **Caption C5** (0:27 to 0:34): Then effort follows risk. How far due diligence goes for each customer rests on an individual risk analysis that takes that assessment into account. Lower risk: the firm may simplify. Increased risk: enhanced measures apply.
- **Caption C6** (0:34 to 0:40): In this example, Customer B is rated higher and gets more: questions on the source of funds, senior management approval, closer monitoring. Customer A gets less, but monitoring must still be enough to spot unusual transactions.
- **Sound**: tick per block; a lower tone when B's warm blocks land.

### S5 · Show why · 0:40 to 0:46

- **Camera**: slow push in (z 1.05 → 1.2) on a strip that unrolls beneath both cards.
- **Visual**: a decision record types on, one row per customer: `A · lower · reasons · date ·
  who decided` and `B · higher · reasons · date · who decided`. A small circular arrow appears at
  the end of each row with the label `revisit when circumstances change`. The radar inset gets
  the same arrow (the assessment is kept up to date).
- **Kinetic type**: header `03 · SHOW WHY`, headline `Write down why.`
- **Caption C7**: The firm must at all times be able to show its supervisor that its measures fit the risks. So each decision is recorded with its reasons, and revisited when circumstances change.
- **Sound**: typewriter ticks (very soft), one light tone at the arrow.

### S6 · What goes wrong · 0:46 to 0:55

- **Camera**: cut-like fast move (0.6 s, cam ease with 0.22 dip) to a wide map tile of the new
  country, z 0.9. The radar inset dims and its document card shows `Last reviewed: before the
  plan changed` (warm).
- **Visual (0:46 to 0:50)**: a cluster of new cards lights up in the new country. Many small warm
  comets arrive from different directions (different payers) and, within a beat, leave as one
  comet per card towards the frame edge labelled `abroad`. A mono label follows the pattern:
  `many payers in · out abroad the same day` and below it `one example: a common money mule pattern`.
  HUD dot turns warm.
- **Visual (0:50 to 0:55)**: the map dims; three warm flag chips slide in, left aligned, under the
  label `COMMON WARNING SIGNS`: `Assessment nobody updates` · `Nearly every customer on the same
  rating` · `Management body never saw it`. A fourth line, cool-grey, under a thin rule:
  `Controls that do not fit the risks → supervisory measures` and `Serious, repeated or systematic
  breaches → fines`.
- **Kinetic type**: header `WHAT GOES WRONG` (accent warm), headline `Risk moves faster than paperwork.`
  (judgement wording, see ledger row 22).
- **Caption C8** (0:46 to 0:50): What goes wrong, in this example: the card launches with no fresh assessment. New cards are loaded by many different payers and emptied abroad the same day, a common money mule pattern.
- **Caption C9** (0:50 to 0:55): Common warning signs: an assessment nobody updates, nearly every customer on the same rating, a management body that never saw it. Controls that do not fit the risks can lead to supervisory measures, and serious, repeated or systematic breaches to fines.
- **Sound**: rapid ticks for incoming comets, tocks for outgoing; a low tone at the warning chips.

### S7 · End card · 0:55 to 0:58

- **Camera**: everything dims to 32 %; hold.
- **Kinetic type**: `Effort follows risk.` / `Show why.` (`why.` in cool). RegRadar mark and
  wordmark rise in. Mono footers: `AMLR · Regulation (EU) 2024/1624 · applies from 10 July 2027`
  and `Fictional example. Not legal advice.`
- **Caption C10**: Effort follows risk, and you can show why. In the EU, the main rules for firms are in the AMLR, Regulation (EU) 2024/1624, which applies from 10 July 2027.
- **Sound**: end chord.
