# Review: PR 66 (academy/records-data), topic "Records, personal data and information sharing" (AMLR Arts 75 to 78)

Reviewer: CHECK stream (independent; did not write the content). Date: 2026-11-25. Branch checked: `origin/academy/records-data` against `origin/main`.
Primary text: `docs/data/laws/32024R1624_EN.json`, Arts 75, 76, 77, 78 read in full. No outside sources used.

## Overall verdict: ready after fixes
The core claims are accurate: five years from the end of the relationship, deletion of personal data at expiry, not redacted, answering the FIU for the five years before the enquiry, no commercial use, human intervention. Five items need rewording. None is a wrong rule, but two could mislead (the card on what may be shared, and the "health data" safeguards citation). The decide scenario's best option is defensible but incomplete.
