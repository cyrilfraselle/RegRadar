## Problem
What hurts today, for whom, in one paragraph. Concrete, not generic.

## Why now
Regulatory or product trigger, with source URL(s). Omit if none.

## Idea
What the tool or feature does, in plain language.

## Smallest prototype
The thinnest static slice that would let the owner judge it in five
minutes, and what demo data it would use (label it as demo).

## Risks and open questions
Legal accuracy, data availability, overlap with existing tools,
anything the owner must decide.

## How we would know it works
