## Transcript (for the page, subtitles file and accessibility)

The AMLR has 90 articles. Which duties are yours? Max starts from the law,
word for word: here, Article 42(1), which applies from 10 July 2027. One:
each point becomes a duty: senior management approval; source of wealth and
source of funds; enhanced, ongoing monitoring. Two: each duty gets a control,
an owner and a status. No control, no test: Max shows the gap. Three: log
each test, with its evidence. Every step links back to the article. One topic,
four layers: the law, AMLA, supervisors, your controls. Deadlines, each with
its source. Training, with a record you can print. From the law to the
control. In one place. Max. Prototype with sample data. Not legal advice.

## Audio description line (for the player's aria-label)

Animated explainer. A stack of law pages; one article splits into three duty
cards that land on a work board next to controls, one with no control shown
as a gap; a test is logged and a line traces it back to the article; then the
topic view, a deadlines calendar and a training certificate. Captions carry
all the words.
