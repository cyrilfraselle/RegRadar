# Pitch (for the owner)

1. A 48-second 3D film that follows one real article, AMLR Art. 42(1), as it splits along its points (a), (b) and (c) into three duties that land on a work board with a control, an owner and a status.
2. One duty has no control and glows amber. Then a test is logged with its evidence, and a glowing trail runs back to the article: "From the law to the control. In one place."
3. Every word on screen is either verbatim law or a claim we can point to in the repo. The film is marked as a prototype with sample data, shows no company name, and its music and effects are generated in the browser and cut on a 120 BPM grid.
