## (f) CEO note: "verified obligation"
An obligation is a duty written in law, for example "firm must do X" in a specific article.
"Verified" here means its title and article were checked against the parsed legal text by script.
Verified is not the same as "in force today" (F-019): dates matter too.
A register is only as credible as its citations; one wrong article harms trust more than a gap.
Hence agents may add obligations only from the stored law text, never from memory.
