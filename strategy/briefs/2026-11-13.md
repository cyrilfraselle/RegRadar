## (f) CEO note: "applies from" date
A law can be adopted, then apply later; duties bind firms only from the application date.
Each obligation in the register needs that date, taken from the law's own text.
F-019 shows all 12 carry 10 Jul 2027, so nothing is "in force today" yet.
A compliance tool that mislabels dates will be challenged by the first MLRO who uses it.
