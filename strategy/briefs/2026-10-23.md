## (f) CEO note: "verified" obligations
Five lines: An obligation is a single duty written in the law (for example a customer due diligence step in AMLR). RegRadar calls it verified when its title and quoted text are checked by script against the parsed official text, so nothing is paraphrased from memory. This is the product's core promise, and why the count (12) matters more than page count. Adding obligations is slow because each must match the legal text. A wrong or invented citation costs far more trust than a missing row.
