Rules

  • Link rr-ui.css before your page style. Your page CSS adds layout only. Never redefine colours, buttons or type; use the tokens.
  • One dark band per page. The page title sits in header.head.rr-band; the work sits below on paper (or on dark paper in dark mode).
  • Blue means link, focus or "live". The primary button is an ink pill (light pill in dark), as on the landing page. Red, amber and green are for state only.
  • No labels above headings, no section numbers, no emoji as icons. A heading carries its own weight.
  • Quality floor: text 12 px or more, contrast 4.5:1 in light and dark, 44 px targets, visible focus, motion off under reduced motion, no sideways scroll at 390, 768 and 1280 px.
  • Floating things stay clear of Max (bottom right, z-index 90). Toasts sit bottom centre at 95; dialogs use native <dialog> and sit above both.
