    Limits of this tool

    • It is a teaching tool, not legal advice. A lawyer must confirm any conclusion about a real customer.
    • Registers and documents can disagree with what you enter. Verify the information and report discrepancies as the AMLR requires; this tool does not.
    • The 25% threshold is the one in Art. 52(1). Member States may end up with lower thresholds for some categories of entity after delegated acts under Art. 52(2); change the threshold above to test a scenario.
    • Nominee arrangements and trusts and similar arrangements (Arts 55 to 58) are not computed. Use the control table to record what you know and read those articles.
    • Art. 54 is applied to the customer's direct holders, and nested cases are worked out step by step. The reading of the article used here is explained in each result and needs a legal fact-check before you rely on it.
    • Control by other means is what you tell the tool. It cannot see agreements, family ties or influence that you do not enter.
