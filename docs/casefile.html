The Casefile

A queue tells you to decide. A casefile makes you find out first. Each investigation gives you a fixed number of analyst hours and more lines of enquiry than you can afford. What you choose to look at determines what you are able to conclude — and if you never check the thing that mattered, you will make a confident, well-reasoned, wrong call, exactly as happens on a real desk.
