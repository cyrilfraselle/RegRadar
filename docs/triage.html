Module 04 · Alert triage

It's Monday.
Six alerts, and a queue behind them.

This is the job. Not the theory of the job — the job. A queue that never empties, mostly noise, with something real hiding in it. Fifteen minutes on the clock.

For each alert you close it, escalate it, or go back and ask for more. Every choice costs something. Escalate everything and you bury the investigations team so the real case sits in a backlog for six weeks. Close everything and you are the reason a fine gets issued.

You are scored the way a model is scored. Precision: of the alerts you escalated, how many were genuinely suspicious. Recall: of the genuinely suspicious ones, how many you caught. Those two numbers fight each other, and learning to hold both is the entire craft. A missed case costs you far more than a wasted escalation — as it should.
