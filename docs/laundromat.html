REGRADAR ACADEMY · MODULE 01 · PLAY THE ADVERSARY

You have €10,000,000
and it is all dirty.

Get as much of it as you can into the legitimate economy before the compliance function closes the net. Every euro must survive three stages — placement, layering, integration. Every operation leaves a trace. The monitoring never sleeps, and the heat you accumulate never goes away.

HOUSE RULES
01Three strikes. Each frozen tranche brings the investigation closer. The third opens a file with your name on it — run over.
02Heat only rises. Successful operations leave footprints too. Lying low slows your visible tempo, but the record does not shrink.
03Size rings bells. Maxing a channel is exponentially louder than using it discreetly. Speed, repetition and risky jurisdictions all compound.
04Every catch teaches. When you are caught, you see the red flag that tripped you and the AMLR article that put that control there. That is the point of the module: you cannot recognise a pattern you have never produced.
05You can walk away. Banking what is already clean is a decision real launderers get wrong. So do analysts.
