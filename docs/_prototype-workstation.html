AML Policy v4.2 · Section 4

Enhanced Due Diligence triggers

Last reviewed 3 January 2026 · Owner: Compliance

A customer or transaction must be escalated to EDD when any of the following apply:

  • Invoice or contract values 40% or more above comparable market pricing for the goods or service described.
  • Payment routed through a jurisdiction not party to the underlying trade (goods shipped A→B, payment routed via C).
  • Beneficial ownership involves a trust, nominee, or foreign holding structure with no public register.
  • Related parties (family members, co-directors) hold combined stakes near the 25% threshold without a shareholders' agreement on file.
  • Customer's expected activity profile has not been reviewed in the preceding 24 months.

Where a trigger applies, the analyst must document the rationale in the case file before the relationship can proceed or continue.
