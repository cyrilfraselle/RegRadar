Annual refresh

The business-wide risk assessment must be kept up to date and regularly reviewed, including after events that significantly change your risk (AMLR Art. 10(2)). The interval below is your own policy; the date is your next planned review.

Current assessment and sign-off

Under AMLR Art. 10(2) the assessment is drawn up by the compliance officer, approved by the management body in its management function and, where such a body exists, communicated to the management body in its supervisory function. Fill in the fields, then save a version to freeze it.

Supervisory function
Freezes the register, scores, sign-off and actions as they are now. If an approval date is set, the next refresh date moves to that date plus your interval.

Saved versions

Each version is a frozen copy you can open as a board report or use as the baseline for the trend.
